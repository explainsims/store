Lamplight 1.1.0
Reading help for students learning English, in Chrome and Edge. Lamplight
reads any page aloud and lights up each word, explains a word with a
picture, and translates into Vietnamese, Korean, Chinese or Japanese.


WHAT'S IN THIS FOLDER

  • Lamplight.app: the app (it carries the browser extension)
  • Install Lamplight.command: installs it (run this)
  • Read Me.txt: this file


BEFORE YOU START

  • Any Mac (Apple Silicon or Intel) running macOS 14 or later. It was made and tested
    on macOS 27.
  • Google Chrome 138 or newer, or Microsoft Edge 148 or newer. They
    translate on this computer, with no internet needed once your
    language has downloaded.


INSTALL

  1. Double-click "Install Lamplight.command".

     If macOS says it can't be opened ("Apple could not verify…"): click
     Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click
     "Open Anyway", then double-click the installer again.

     Or open Terminal, type  bash  followed by a space, drag
     "Install Lamplight.command" into the Terminal window, and press Return.

  2. The installer moves Lamplight into Applications and opens it. It lives
     in the menu bar (the little lamp).

  3. Add Lamplight's extension to Chrome (and Edge, if you use it). It's free
     and doesn't need the Web Store:
       • Open chrome://extensions (edge://extensions in Edge).
       • Turn on Developer mode.
       • Click Load unpacked and choose the folder Lamplight shows you:
         ~/Library/Application Support/Lamplight/Extension
         Quickest way: click Copy Path in Lamplight's window. In the
         window Load unpacked opens, press ⌘⇧G (Command-Shift-G),
         then ⌘V to paste, then Return, and click Select.
     A welcome page opens. Pick your language, click "Get" to download it
     for translating (once), and listen to the voices.


USING Lamplight

  • The toolbar: click the lamp in the browser's toolbar and choose "Show
    the toolbar on this page", or turn on "Show on every website" once.
    It's always there in Google Docs. Drag it by the lamp to any corner.
    ⌥⇧L (Option-Shift-L) shows or hides it.

  • Read aloud: press Play, or select some text and press ⌥⇧R. Each word
    lights up as it's read. Hover (the pointer button) reads any sentence
    you rest the pointer on.

  • Look up a word: double-click it. The card shows a picture, how to say
    it, what it means, the word in your language, and the sentence it came
    from. Press "Add to word bank" to keep it.

  • Translate: select a sentence and press Translate. Listen to it in
    English or in your language.

  • The reader: the page button opens the page, a Google Doc or a PDF in a
    calm view, with your language under each paragraph. Choose the font
    (Lexend, Atkinson Hyperlegible, OpenDyslexic), size, spacing, page
    colour and a reading ruler.

  • Word bank: the star button. Practise your words (the ones due come
    back on their own, sooner if you missed them), copy them into a Google
    Sheet, or save them as a CSV.

  • Talk and type: click in a text box, then the microphone, and speak.
    The switch on the microphone bubble chooses English or your own
    language. The first time, macOS asks to let Lamplight use the microphone and
    speech recognition: click Allow (or turn them on in System Settings →
    Privacy & Security → Microphone and → Speech Recognition). In Google
    Docs the microphone starts Docs' own Voice typing.

  • Better voices: Lamplight reads with the Mac's own voices. For clearer
    ones, open System Settings, search for "Spoken Content", then System
    voice → Manage Voices…, and add a Premium or Enhanced voice for your
    language. Lamplight's window shows which voices this Mac has.


GOOD TO KNOW

  • Nothing leaves your computer. Reading, translating, looking up words
    and talk and type all happen on this Mac. Lamplight has no account,
    no server and no tracking.

  • Talk and type needs Lamplight running. It opens at login on its own
    (Launch at Login in its menu turns that off). Everything else works
    without it.

  • To update: use Check for Updates… in Lamplight's menu, or unzip a newer
    copy and run its installer. The extension updates itself from the same
    folder.

  • To uninstall: remove Lamplight from chrome://extensions, then quit
    Lamplight and drag it from Applications to the Trash.

  • Lamplight is free and open source. It isn't from the App Store and isn't
    notarized by Apple, which is why macOS asks you to confirm it the
    first time. It's an open-source alternative to Read&Write for Google
    Chrome, and isn't affiliated with Texthelp or with Google.
