Equate 1.0.0
Snip an equation anywhere on your screen and paste it as LaTeX, as a real
equation in Word or Google Docs, as plain text or as a picture. Equate reads
it on your Mac: no account, no limit, nothing is uploaded.


WHAT'S IN THIS FOLDER

  • Equate.app: the app
  • Install Equate.command: installs it (run this)
  • Read Me.txt: this file


BEFORE YOU START

  • Any Mac (Apple Silicon or Intel) running macOS 14 or later.


INSTALL

  1. Double-click "Install Equate.command".

     If macOS says it can't be opened ("Apple could not verify…"): click
     Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click
     "Open Anyway", then double-click the installer again.

     Or open Terminal, type  bash  followed by a space, drag
     "Install Equate.command" into the Terminal window, and press Return.

  2. The installer moves Equate into Applications and opens it.

  Prefer to do it by hand? Drag Equate into Applications and open it. The
  first time, macOS blocks it: go to System Settings → Privacy & Security
  and click "Open Anyway".


USING Equate

  Equate lives in the menu bar (the blue square with an equals sign).

  • Snip: press Control-Option-E, then drag a box round an equation in a
    PDF, a web page or a picture. Esc cancels.

  • A panel shows what Equate read, next to your snip. It's already on
    the clipboard in the best form for the app you were in: just press
    Command-V. That's a real equation in Word and Google Docs, a sharp
    picture in Pages and Keynote, and $$ LaTeX $$ in Obsidian and Notion.

  • Want another format? The panel's buttons copy LaTeX (Command-1), a
    Word equation (Command-2), a Google Docs equation (Command-3), MathML
    (Command-4), plain text like μₛ and x² (Command-5) or a picture
    (Command-6).

  • Check it: a green badge means two readers agreed. An orange one shows
    the other reading, and lets you swap a doubtful symbol (μ or P?) in
    one click. You can also edit the LaTeX in the panel.

  • More ways in, from the menu-bar menu:
      - Snip Again (Control-Option-Shift-E) reads the same spot again.
      - Write with Trackpad (Control-Option-W): write with your mouse, or
        click "Write with Finger" and write with one finger on the
        trackpad.
      - Open Image or PDF: pick a PDF page and drag a box on it.
      - Take Photo with iPhone: photograph the whiteboard.
      - You can also drop a picture or PDF onto the menu-bar icon.

  • History keeps every equation. Search it, copy one again, or drag one
    into a document.

  • Settings: change the shortcuts, what each app gets when you paste,
    LaTeX delimiters, picture size, and Launch at Login.

  Permission: the first snip asks for Screen Recording. Turn on Equate in
  System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording,
  then snip again (macOS may ask you to reopen Equate).

  On macOS 27 with Apple Intelligence on, Apple's own reader gives a
  second opinion. Equate works without it.


GOOD TO KNOW

  • To update: unzip a newer copy and run its installer. It replaces the
    old one and keeps your settings.

  • To uninstall: quit Equate and drag it from Applications to the Trash.

  • Equate is free and open source. It isn't from the App Store and isn't
    notarized by Apple, which is why macOS asks you to confirm it the
    first time.
