Figure It 0.5.10
Figures for tests and worksheets: 73 of them, for Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Maths
and Economics. Change the numbers and labels, make a student copy and an answer key,
then drag the figure into Pages, Keynote or Google Docs.


WHAT'S IN THIS FOLDER

  • Figure It.app: the app
  • Install Figure It.command: installs it (run this)
  • Read Me.txt: this file


BEFORE YOU START

  • Any Mac (Apple Silicon or Intel) running macOS 14 or later.


INSTALL

  1. Double-click "Install Figure It.command".

     If macOS says it can't be opened ("Apple could not verify…"): click
     Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click
     "Open Anyway", then double-click the installer again.

     Or open Terminal, type  bash  followed by a space, drag
     "Install Figure It.command" into the Terminal window, and press Return.

  2. The installer moves Figure It into Applications and opens it.

  Prefer to do it by hand? Drag Figure It into Applications and open it. The
  first time, macOS blocks it: go to System Settings → Privacy & Security
  and click "Open Anyway".


USING Figure It

  1. Pick a figure. Home shows every figure as a picture; click one. Or
     search on the left, or open a subject there.

  2. Change it. The settings are on the right: angles, lengths, labels,
     forces, data. Type a label's text in its box. Words like theta or
     F_N become θ and F with a small N.

  3. Put it in your test. Drag the figure into Pages, Keynote, Word or
     Google Docs, or click Copy (⌘C) and paste. Export saves a PDF, PNG
     or SVG file.

  Student copy and answer key
    Click Ask beside a label you want students to work out. The Student
    Copy leaves it as a blank line; the Answer Key shows it in red. Switch
    between them in the bar above the figure. Copy and drag send the one
    showing.

  Student versions A, B and C
    Click the die beside each number that should change between versions,
    then click Randomize (⌘R). Use one version, or keep all three.

  My Figures
    Every figure you change is kept in My Figures, in iCloud Drive, so your
    other Mac has it too. Recent shows them all. New Folder (under My
    Figures) keeps a unit or a test together: drag figures in. Right-click
    a figure to rename, move or delete it; right-click a folder to rename
    or delete it. Select (or ⌘-click) picks several figures at once.
    A PDF, PNG or SVG made by Figure It opens again for editing: open it
    with Figure It, or drop it onto the figure area.

  Menu bar
    The ramp icon in the menu bar starts a figure, or drags a recent one
    into a document, without opening the window. Turn it off in Settings.

  Settings (⌘,)
    Your test's font, label size and line weight, so figures match the
    rest of the paper, and a keyboard shortcut for a quick figure.

  Figure It needs no special permissions and doesn't use the internet.


GOOD TO KNOW

  • To update: use Storefront, or unzip a newer copy and run its installer.
    It replaces the old one and keeps your figures and settings.

  • To uninstall: quit Figure It and drag it from Applications to the Trash.
    Your figures stay in iCloud Drive › Figure It.

  • The figures come from teacher.dev's open-source figure generators
    (Apache License 2.0). Figure It itself is MIT licensed. The licences are
    inside the app (Contents/Resources).

  • Figure It is free and open source. It isn't from the App Store and isn't
    notarized by Apple, which is why macOS asks you to confirm it the
    first time.
