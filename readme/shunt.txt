Shunt 1.0.0
Move the window you're using, or just the browser tab you're looking at,
to another Space or another screen with one shortcut.


WHAT'S IN THIS FOLDER

  • Shunt.app: the app
  • Install Shunt.command: installs it (run this)
  • Read Me.txt: this file


BEFORE YOU START

  • Any Mac (Apple Silicon or Intel) running macOS 14 or later.


INSTALL

  1. Double-click "Install Shunt.command".

     If macOS says it can't be opened ("Apple could not verify…"): click
     Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click
     "Open Anyway", then double-click the installer again.

     Or open Terminal, type  bash  followed by a space, drag
     "Install Shunt.command" into the Terminal window, and press Return.

  2. The installer moves Shunt into Applications and opens it.

  Prefer to do it by hand? Drag Shunt into Applications and open it. The
  first time, macOS blocks it: go to System Settings → Privacy & Security
  and click "Open Anyway".


USING Shunt

  Shunt lives in the menu bar (the little blue tile with a window on a
  rail). The first time, it asks for one permission:

    System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility → turn on Shunt.

  It also uses Mission Control's own "Move left a space" and "Move right a
  space" shortcuts, so leave those on (System Settings → Keyboard →
  Keyboard Shortcuts → Mission Control). They're on unless you changed them.

  The keys:

    ⌃⌘ ← →     the window goes one Space left or right
    ⌃⌘ ↑       the window goes to the other screen (a monitor, or an
               AirPlay TV set to Extend)
    ⌃⌘ ↓       back to the Mac's own screen
    ⌃⌘ 1–9     to Desktop 1–9
    add ⇧      just the browser tab (Chrome, Edge, Safari)
    ⌃⌘ Z       undo the last move

  Hold ⌃⌘ and press an arrow more than once to go further. A small map
  shows where it went. Change any key in Settings (click the menu-bar icon,
  then Settings…).

  The menu-bar icon also shows your Spaces: click one to send the window
  you were using there, or click a window under "Bring here" to fetch it
  from another Space.

  "Go with it" leaves you where the window went; "Stay here" slides over
  and straight back. A tab sent to a TV fills it, and your Mac keeps the
  focus. With no TV connected, ⌃⌘↑ offers to open Throw so you can pick
  one.


GOOD TO KNOW

  • To update: click the menu-bar icon, then Check for Updates…, or unzip
    a newer copy and run its installer. It keeps your settings.

  • To uninstall: quit Shunt from its menu-bar icon and drag it from
    Applications to the Trash.

  • Shunt is free and open source. It isn't from the App Store and isn't
    notarized by Apple, which is why macOS asks you to confirm it the
    first time.
