Haul 1.0.0
Save video and audio from YouTube and well over a thousand other sites.
Paste or drop a link, and Haul downloads it in the quality you like as
a file that plays in QuickTime, Keynote, PowerPoint and on iPhones.

Only download what you have the right to keep: your own videos, Creative
Commons and public-domain films, lectures shared for study. Haul never
gets around copy protection.


WHAT'S IN THIS FOLDER

  • Haul.app: the app
  • Install Haul.command: installs it (run this)
  • Read Me.txt: this file


BEFORE YOU START

  • Any Mac (Apple Silicon or Intel) running macOS 14 or later. It was made and tested
    on macOS 27.
  • An internet connection the first time it opens: Haul downloads its
    download engine (yt-dlp, about 55 MB) and keeps it up to date.


INSTALL

  1. Double-click "Install Haul.command".

     If macOS says it can't be opened ("Apple could not verify…"): click
     Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click
     "Open Anyway", then double-click the installer again.

     Or open Terminal, type  bash  followed by a space, drag
     "Install Haul.command" into the Terminal window, and press Return.

  2. The installer moves Haul into Applications and opens it.

  Prefer to do it by hand? Drag Haul into Applications and open it. The
  first time, macOS blocks it: go to System Settings → Privacy & Security
  and click "Open Anyway".


USING Haul

  • Paste a link in the bar at the top and press Download. Or drop a link
    on the window, the Dock icon or the menu-bar icon.
  • ⌥⌘V downloads the link on the clipboard. Settings › General can give
    that its own shortcut that works in any app.
  • The quality menu next to the bar sets Best, a size, Audio only or Ask.
    Hold ⌥ when you press Download to choose the size, cut out just a part
    (2:10 to 5:45), or save a transcript.
  • A playlist link asks which videos you want.
  • Pause, resume and cancel with the buttons on each row. Downloads carry
    on after you quit and reopen, and wait by themselves when Wi-Fi drops.
  • When a page won't download, Find on a Page (⌘E) opens it in a browser
    window: press play and pick what it found.
  • For private, members-only or age-restricted videos, Sign In… (⌥⌘L) once
    in Haul's own browser. Safari, Chrome and Edge never see it.
  • History (⇧⌘Y) keeps everything you've downloaded. Search… (⌘F)
    searches YouTube and SoundCloud.
  • Browser extension for Chrome and Edge: Settings › Browser Extension
    shows the three steps (Load unpacked, no Web Store).
  • Agent Access (Settings) lets Claude Code use Haul on this Mac.

  Permissions: macOS may ask once to let Haul send notifications, and,
  if you turn on "Add to Music or TV", to control Music or TV.


GOOD TO KNOW

  • To update: Haul › Check for Updates…, or unzip a newer copy and run
    its installer. It keeps your settings. The download engine updates
    itself every day (Settings › Updates).

  • To uninstall: quit Haul and drag it from Applications to the Trash.

  • Haul is free and open source, an open-source alternative to Downie
    (not affiliated with it). It isn't from the App Store and isn't
    notarized by Apple, which is why macOS asks you to confirm it the
    first time.
