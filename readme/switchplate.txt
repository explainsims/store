Switchplate 1.0.0
A menu-bar app with four handy jobs: keep the Mac awake (even with the lid
closed, on battery too), one-click switches like dark mode and Empty Trash,
quit apps when you close their last window, and lock the keyboard while
you clean it.


WHAT'S IN THIS FOLDER

  • Switchplate.app: the app
  • Install Switchplate.command: installs it (run this)
  • Read Me.txt: this file


BEFORE YOU START

  • Any Mac (Apple Silicon or Intel) running macOS 14 or later.


INSTALL

  1. Double-click "Install Switchplate.command".

     If macOS says it can't be opened ("Apple could not verify…"): click
     Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click
     "Open Anyway", then double-click the installer again.

     Or open Terminal, type  bash  followed by a space, drag
     "Install Switchplate.command" into the Terminal window, and press Return.

  2. The installer moves Switchplate into Applications and opens it.

  Prefer to do it by hand? Drag Switchplate into Applications and open it. The
  first time, macOS blocks it: go to System Settings → Privacy & Security
  and click "Open Anyway".


USING Switchplate

  Switchplate lives in the menu bar: a small blue tile with a light switch.
  Click it for the menu. The top switch in the icon glows orange while
  the Mac is being kept awake.

  KEEP AWAKE
    Switch it on, or pick 15 min, 1 hour, 2 hours, Until… or Always.
    ⌥-click the menu-bar icon to switch it on or off without the menu.
    In Settings → Keep awake it can also come on by itself: while chosen
    apps are open, on power, with a TV or second screen, or while Throw
    presents. The menu also says which other apps are keeping the Mac
    awake.

    "Also with the lid closed" lets you close the lid and carry the Mac
    while things keep running, on battery too. It needs a one-time OK:
    click Set Up…, then in System Settings → General → Login Items &
    Extensions, turn on Switchplate under "Allow in the Background". It
    stops by itself when the battery falls below 20% (you can change
    this) or if the Mac gets too hot. A closed Mac in a bag gets warm.
    While it's on, the Mac doesn't sleep from the Apple menu either:
    turn Keep awake off first.

  QUICK TOGGLES
    One click each: Dark mode, Hide desktop icons, Eject disks, Empty
    Trash, Lock screen, Night Shift, Do Not Disturb, Mute mic, Sleep
    screen, Hide Dock, Clean keyboard, Screen saver. Empty Trash always
    asks first, because it can't be undone. Choose which tiles show,
    their order and a shortcut for each in Settings → Quick toggles.

  QUIT ON CLOSE
    Switch it on and choose apps (Preview, say). When you close an app's
    last window, a notice says "Quitting…" for a few seconds; click Keep
    It Open to stop it. Windows on other desktops and minimised windows
    still count. In Settings → Quit on close you can also quit any app
    that has had no windows for an hour (mail and chat apps are left
    alone).

  CLEAN KEYBOARD
    Click the Clean keyboard tile, choose the keyboard (or keyboard and
    trackpad), a black screen or a small notice, and how long. To stop
    early, type  done . The power button and closing the lid always stop
    it too.

  PERMISSIONS
    Switchplate asks the first time a feature needs it:
    • Accessibility (Quit on close, Clean keyboard, Do Not Disturb):
      System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility → Switchplate.
    • Control Finder (Empty Trash) and System Events (Hide Dock): macOS
      asks with a button; say OK.


GOOD TO KNOW

  • To update: use "Check for Updates…" in the menu, or unzip a newer
    copy and run its installer. Your settings stay.

  • To uninstall: quit Switchplate (in its menu) and drag it from
    Applications to the Trash.

  • Switchplate is free and open source. It isn't from the App Store and isn't
    notarized by Apple, which is why macOS asks you to confirm it the
    first time.
