Footprints privacy
Footprints works entirely on your own computer. It has no account, no ads and
no analytics, and there's no Footprints server: nothing about you, your
students or their documents is ever sent to us or anyone else. Last updated
14 November 2026.


WHAT FOOTPRINTS READS

  • A Google Doc's saved history, only when you ask: when you click the
    Footprints button in a doc, open a replay, or check a class. It asks
    Google Docs for the history as you, from inside your browser, the same
    way Google Docs' own version history does. Google only shares it with
    people who can edit the doc.
  • Links on the page you're on, only when you click "Check every doc on
    this page", to find the Google Docs it links to.
  • Footprints can't see your other tabs, your browsing, or any site other
    than Google Docs.


WHAT FOOTPRINTS KEEPS

  • Histories it has read, so they open quickly next time and only new
    saves are read. They're kept in the browser's storage on this computer,
    for 30 days unless you change it in Settings, where "Forget all" deletes
    them at once. You can also choose to keep nothing.
  • A fingerprint of each doc it has read (short codes for runs of words),
    used to find text shared between docs. It's kept and deleted with the
    histories.
  • Your settings, in the browser on this computer.
  • PDF reports and CSV files only go where you save them.


THE MAC APP

  • Footprints.app only puts the extension in a folder on your Mac and
    shows how to add it to Chrome and Edge.
  • It goes online only to check for updates (store.explainsims.com), and
    sends nothing about you when it does.


GOOD TO KNOW

  • Footprints is free and open source.
  • A paste or a short session isn't proof of anything on its own: use what
    Footprints shows to start a conversation with the writer.
  • Questions: use Feedback on Footprints' page in Storefront.
