Straightedge privacy
Straightedge works entirely on your own computer. It has no account, no ads
and no analytics, and there's no Straightedge server: nothing about you, your
students or their data is ever sent to us or anyone else. Last updated
17 November 2026.


WHAT STRAIGHTEDGE READS

  • The cells you graph, only when you ask: when you click Graph in a
    Google Sheet, press ⌥⇧G, or click Straightedge in the toolbar. It reads
    them inside your browser the way a copy would, without touching your
    clipboard, so it can only read what you could copy yourself.
  • While its panel is open beside a sheet, the same cells again after you
    edit them, so the graph keeps up. It never reads other cells unless you
    ask it to add columns beside your data.
  • Which cells are selected in a sheet (not what's in them), so its
    panel can offer to graph a new selection.
  • Straightedge can't see your other tabs, your browsing, or any site
    other than Google Sheets.


WHAT STRAIGHTEDGE KEEPS

  • Each sheet's last graph (which cells, the columns and transforms you
    chose, the words on the graph), so it opens the same way next time.
    It's kept in the browser's storage on this computer.
  • Your settings, in the browser on this computer.
  • Pictures, SVG files and copied results only go where you put them.


WHAT STRAIGHTEDGE WRITES

  • Nothing in your sheet unless you ask: Put in sheet adds a picture of the
    graph beside your data, and Add the processed columns adds columns of
    formulas there.


THE MAC APP

  • Straightedge.app only puts the extension in a folder on your Mac and
    shows how to add it to Chrome and Edge.
  • It goes online only to check for updates (store.explainsims.com), and
    sends nothing about you when it does.


GOOD TO KNOW
