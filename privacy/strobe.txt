Strobe privacy
Strobe works on your own Mac or iPhone. It has no account of its own, no ads
and no analytics, and there's no Strobe server: your videos and projects stay
on your device unless you save them somewhere yourself. Last updated 14
November 2026.


WHAT STROBE KEEPS

  • Projects: .strobe files, with the video inside, wherever you save them:
    on your Mac, in the iPhone's Strobe folder (Files › On My iPhone ›
    Strobe), or in your Google Drive if you choose to.
  • Settings: on your device only.
  • Your Google sign-in, if you sign in: a sign-in token kept on your device,
    in a file only your user account can read on the Mac and in the Keychain
    on the iPhone. Signing out deletes it and asks Google to cancel it.


GOOGLE DRIVE

  • Signing in with Google is optional. Everything else in Strobe works
    without it.
  • Strobe asks Google for your name and email address, to show which
    account is signed in, and for permission to see and change only the
    Drive files that Strobe makes or that you open with it. Google calls
    this permission drive.file. Strobe can't see the rest of your Drive.
  • Strobe uses that permission only to make a Strobe folder in your Drive,
    list the projects in it, download a project you open and upload a
    project you save.
  • Browse Google Drive shows Google's own file picker inside Strobe, so you
    can open a project that Strobe didn't make. Strobe gets only the file
    you pick.
  • Strobe talks to Google straight from your device. Nothing goes through
    us, and we never see your Google account, your files or your sign-in.
  • We don't sell, share or transfer Google user data, use it for ads, or
    let anyone read it. Strobe's use of information received from Google
    APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the
    Limited Use requirements.
  • You can take Strobe's access away at any time: sign out in Strobe, or
    remove Strobe at myaccount.google.com › Security › Your connections to
    third-party apps and services.


CAMERA AND PHOTOS

  • Camera (iPhone): to record experiments. Recordings go into Strobe's
    folder on your iPhone, and a copy goes to your Photos library if you
    allow it.
  • Photos: to open a video you pick from your Photos library, at its full
    frame rate. Strobe reads only the videos you pick.
  • The iPhone's level uses its motion sensor while the camera is open.
    Nothing is recorded or kept.


THE INTERNET

  • Strobe goes online only for Google sign-in and Google Drive, when you
    use them, and on the Mac to check for updates on store.explainsims.com
    (Strobe › Check for Updates…). That check sends Strobe's version, like
    any app update check, and nothing about you or your projects.
